The week ahead

Each night's sun and moon, worked out the same way as the chart. On the bars, bone is twilight or moonlight and the dotted stretches are proper dark. We cannot see cloud from a web page. If the sky is closing we text you by 3 PM, and you pick another night or take a refund.

    Two tours, and the moon decides which

    Eight guests at most, two guides, three telescopes. We meet forty minutes after sunset so your eyes adjust while the sky finishes going dark.

    Moon and planets

    2 hours $65 a seat

    Runs when the moon is up in the evening. Craters along the shadow line, Saturn's rings, and double stars that split into two colors. Fine for children from 7 up.

    Deep sky

    3 hours $95 a seat

    Runs when the evening is moonless. The Andromeda galaxy, star clusters, and from late autumn the Orion nebula once it clears the hills. Ages 10 and up. It gets cold and it runs late.

    The whole night, private

    Up to 6 people $720

    Dusk until you have had enough, on any night we are free. Write to us and we will find the darkest date near yours.

    What we bring

    • A 16-inch reflector on a rocker base. We call her Abuela.
    • Two 8-inch reflectors that anyone can steer.
    • Binoculars on tripods, camp chairs and wool blankets.
    • Hot chocolate, and a red torch for every guest.

    What you bring

    • Closed shoes. The lot is gravel and the cholla is close.
    • One more layer than seems sensible. November nights out here drop into the 40s.
    • Water, and patience for the first twenty minutes.
    • No white lights. Turn your phone screen right down.

    Who takes you

    Inez Varela taught high school physics for eleven years and has kept a log of this sky since 2016. Every tour has a second guide, so there is always someone at each telescope.

    Phoenix glows low in the south, and we do not pretend otherwise. Overhead and to the north it is properly dark.

    Good to know

    • Tours run Tuesday to Sunday. Mondays the telescopes get cleaned.

    • The lot is flat and both small telescopes work from a seated position. Tell us what you need.

    Find us

    Where we meet

    Cholla Tank staging lot, at the north end of Sidewinder Wash Road in the Rio Verde Foothills. About 35 minutes from Old Town Scottsdale.

    The last two miles are graded dirt. Any car manages it slowly. Park facing out and switch off your headlights when you see the red lamps.
