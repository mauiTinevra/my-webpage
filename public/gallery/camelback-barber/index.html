Who is in which chair

  • Chair 1

    Odell

    Opened the shop in 2009 and still sweeps it. Straight razor work, flat tops and tapers that grow out clean. Will tell you if the cut you asked for is a bad idea.

    Tuesday to Saturday

  • Chair 2

    Tino

    The fade chair. Skin fades, burst fades, crisp line-ups, and a steady hand with a part. Came up cutting his cousins on a porch in Maryvale.

    Tuesday to Saturday

  • Chair 3

    Marisol

    Scissor cuts, longer hair, curls, and the patience for a first haircut. Trained in Tucson, eleven years behind a chair. In from 11 on weekdays.

    Wednesday to Saturday

  • Chair 4

    Wes

    Beards and kids. Shapes a beard so it suits the face it is on, and has talked more than one four-year-old out of a meltdown. Off on Saturdays for his daughter's soccer.

    Tuesday to Friday
