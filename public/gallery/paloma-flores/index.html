      Today's three

      Paloma ties three bunches every morning from whatever came in best. Take one as it is, or load it into the vase and change it.

        Weddings, two a weekend

        We take two weddings each weekend and no more, so the person who met you in March is the one tying your bouquet in October. Most of what we use is grown within forty miles, which means the flowers follow the season and not a catalog.

        Start with a free half hour at the shop. Bring a photo of the room and one of something you love.

        The Saturday flower bar

        Every Saturday from 8 to 1 we roll the buckets out onto the sidewalk and sell by the stem. Walk the row, pull what you like, and we wrap it at the counter for nothing extra.

        • Stems run $2 to $6. Most people leave with about $20 of flowers.
        • Bring your own vase and we will arrange into it while you wait.
        • Kids get one marigold free, their pick.

        No ordering ahead for the bar. It is first come, and the snapdragons go early.
