How we grade

We grade the vinyl under a lamp and play anything over $20 before it gets a sticker.

NM
Near mint. Looks unplayed, plays quiet.
VG+
A few light marks. You'll hear the music, not the record.
VG
Visible wear and some surface noise between songs.
G+
Well loved. Plays through without skipping, with crackle.

If a record plays worse at home than its grade, bring it back within 14 days for the price you paid.

We buy records, one box or a whole garage

We look at every record, tell you what we'd pay for each pile and why, and you're free to say no and take them home.

  1. Bring up to two crates to the counter. Any open day before 5, no appointment. It takes about twenty minutes a crate.
  2. Have more than that? We come to you. Anywhere in the Valley, usually within the week. Call and tell us roughly how many and what kind.
  3. Get paid the same day. Cash, or 30 percent more as store credit. We pay 40 to 50 percent of what we'll price a record at.

Always looking for

Soul, jazz, cumbia and Tejano, punk and metal, private-press oddities, anything recorded in Arizona.

Usually pass on

Water-damaged sleeves, warped or deeply scratched records, and most easy-listening box sets.

Call the buying line, 602-555-0117 buy@ocotillorecords.example

Hear it before you buy it

Two turntables sit by the front window, each with a pair of headphones. Take up to three records over at a time. If one looks dusty, hand it to us and we'll clean it first.

When there's a line, please keep it to fifteen minutes. Sealed records stay sealed.
