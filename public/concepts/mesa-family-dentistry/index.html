Meet your dentist

Dr. David R. Ericksen, DDS

His main focus is educating his patients and building lasting relationships.

Dr. Ericksen was raised in the East Valley and earned his Bachelor of Science at Arizona State University, where he also taught chemistry before attending Loma Linda University School of Dentistry. That teaching background shows in how clearly he explains what's going on in your mouth.

He takes part in local study clubs, has treated underserved communities around the world, and in his free time plays sand volleyball and writes music on his ukulele.
