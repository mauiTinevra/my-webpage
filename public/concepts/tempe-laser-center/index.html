How laser hair removal works

Light targets the pigment in each hair follicle so it stops growing, while the skin around it is left alone.

1

Consult

We look at your hair and skin type and the area you want treated, and plan your sessions.

2

Treat

An upper lip takes seconds. Legs or a back take about 15 to 30 minutes. No numbing gel needed.

3

Repeat

Hair grows in cycles, so a few sessions catch every follicle. Each one leaves hair thinner and slower.
