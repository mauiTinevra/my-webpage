Meet your dentist

Michio Kubota, DDS

Dr. Kubota was born in Ehime, Japan and grew up mostly in Toyota. He attended Waseda University, then Creighton University in Omaha, Nebraska, where he earned a B.S. in Physics and his D.D.S. from the School of Dentistry.

  • 1994Begins practicing in the East Valley, first in Phoenix, then with Bay Shore Dental Group
  • 2006Opens Harmony Dental of AZ in Gilbert
  • TodayMember of the Arizona Dental Association and the American Dental Association
Dr. Kubota puts his family first. He's drawn to the water, from the oceans of Hawaii to Saguaro and Canyon Lakes, where he likes to canoe and kayak after sunset, when he feels most in harmony with nature.
