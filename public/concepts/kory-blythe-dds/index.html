Meet Dr. Blythe

A Scottsdale dentist, born and raised

It has always been my goal to provide my patients with the highest quality care possible. Our practice begins with family.

Dr. Blythe grew up in Arizona, graduating from Scottsdale's Chaparral High School and Arizona State University, and earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Southern California in 1993. He came straight back to the Valley and made Scottsdale the home of his practice.

He's married to Michelle and is the father of two daughters, Alexandra and Kamryn. He coaches girls' volleyball, has played in a Scottsdale men's softball league since 1993, and has won gold medals on a men's senior Olympic volleyball team.
