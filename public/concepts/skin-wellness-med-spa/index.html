About us — Embrace your best self

Rooted in body wisdom & innovation

Sara Kranke

DNP, ACNP-C · Co-founder

Sara is driven by a profound dedication to wellness and illness prevention — the inspiration for a sanctuary where people of all ages can harmonize their inner health and prevent aging externally through a variety of advanced modalities.

She speaks at national conventions and trains medical providers of all experience levels. Her doctoral thesis focused on Reiki as an evidence-based non-pharmaceutical intervention for reducing anxiety and pain during injectable treatments.

Kira Best

Licensed aesthetician · Certified laser technician

Kira has a background in the industry of well over a decade, with experience teaching advanced laser techniques and collaborating with world-renowned plastic surgeons and professionals in the field.

As a Key Opinion Leader and Luminary with Sciton, Kira provides expert training and advice to other skin professionals.
