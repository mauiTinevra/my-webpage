Welcome to Royal

Quality treatments that suit your individual needs.

We strive to provide an environment where all of our guests receive a Royal experience — quality treatments that suit their individual needs, and feeling delighted when leaving. Each client can feel confident that their treatment and plan of care are directed to achieve their desired results in a safe and thorough manner.

We safely administer toxins (Botox, Dysport, Jeuveau, Daxxify) and dermal fillers (Juvéderm, Restylane, RHA) as well as Sculptra & Radiesse, and carry medical-grade skin care for beautiful, glowing esthetic treatments.

Meet the founder

Deborah Peraza, RN

Deborah embarked on her nursing career over 19 years ago and takes great pride in being a 4th generation Arizonian. In 2018 she transitioned from RN Administrator to Aesthetic Nurse Injector, and in 2019 she founded Royal Aesthetics with a vision to create a medspa that exuded luxury and relaxation.

Deborah also offers training programs for injectors — a Foundations Injector Course, an Advanced Injector Course, and an RN Injector Apprenticeship.

“The decision to become an RN Injector has been a true blessing, as I am driven by my desire to provide each patient with results that make them feel cherished and valued.”
