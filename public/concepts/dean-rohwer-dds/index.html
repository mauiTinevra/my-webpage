Meet Dr. Rohwer

A sweet tooth and a sweet heart

Dr. Rohwer graduated from the University of Colorado School of Dentistry and has practiced in Arizona since 1998. He focuses on cosmetic dentistry and computer-guided implants, and keeps learning the newest advances in the field.

His philosophy is simple: healthy teeth and gums matter because they let you speak, eat, smile and enjoy life, including the occasional piece of chocolate.
