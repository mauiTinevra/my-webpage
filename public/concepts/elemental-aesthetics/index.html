Meet Dr. Summer Swanick, NMD

Medical experience, an artist's eye.

Dr. Swanick began her medical career as a primary care physician in private practice, working primarily with weight loss and hormone replacement therapy, before developing a strong interest in aesthetic medicine. She went on to serve as medical director for one of Arizona's largest corporately owned medical spas, performing procedures and supervising doctors, nurses and aestheticians across multiple clinics.

In 2014 she returned to private practice, integrating her experience with aesthetic medicine as a core part of her practice. With over 17 years of experience in art and sculpture, she brings an artistic edge to treating the aging face with dermal fillers such as Voluma, Restylane and Juvederm.

“The true art of anti-aging medicine is to blend transition, to eliminate the shadows. That's how we soften the signs of aging and maintain a ‘natural’ look.”
