Meet your dentist

Dr. Zachary K. Davis, DDS

Dr. Davis grew up in Mesa, earned his biology degree at Arizona State University and his DDS at the University of the Pacific in San Francisco. He's a graduate of the WhiteCap Institute implant training program and keeps current on the latest treatments.

He and his wife Rebecca have five children, so he knows what it takes to get a whole family to the dentist. Away from the office you'll find him camping, wakeboarding, riding dirt bikes or golfing.
