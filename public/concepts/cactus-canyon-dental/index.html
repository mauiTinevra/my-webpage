Meet Dr. Collins

An Arizona native who wanted to be a dentist since she was a child.

Born and raised in Kingman, Dr. Collins earned her BS in Cellular and Molecular Biology at the University of Northern Colorado, where she swam and dove for the university, then graduated from the Oregon Health & Science University School of Dentistry in 2012. In June 2019 she took over Dr. Nancy J. Robinson's practice here in Tempe.

Away from the office she spends time with her husband Ben, their two children and their dog Riggs. She finished AZ Ironman in 2015 and has one continent left to visit: Antarctica.
