The MRD Dental Plan

Our in-house plan helps you and your family save on the care you need, with discounts between 30 and 70% on covered treatment. It is paid with a 12-month agreement.

Specialty services are not covered. Fees shown are from our current plan fee schedule and are subject to change. The plan cannot be combined with insurance. Ask the front desk for full details.
