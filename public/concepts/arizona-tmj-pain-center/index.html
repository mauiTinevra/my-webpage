TMJ · Nerve pain · Headache · Sleep apnea

Jaw and facial pain, carefully treated.

Does this sound familiar?

  • Jaw pain or stiffness, or discomfort while chewing
  • Painful jaw clicking, or a jaw that locks open or closed
  • Headaches and general facial pain
  • Ear pain, ringing or congestion
  • Neck and shoulder pain

These are common symptoms of TMJ disorders. A thorough history and evaluation is the first step.

What we treat

Four areas of focus, one careful evaluation.

Through a thorough history, evaluation and review of diagnostic data, we work with you to find the best possible solutions for these complex, often interconnected conditions.

01

TMJ disorders

Pain and dysfunction in the jaw joint and the muscles that control jaw movement.

02

Nerve pain

Neuropathic pain of the face and mouth, including the trigeminal nerve and phantom tooth pain.

03

Headache

Pain or discomfort anywhere in the head or neck region.

04

Sleep apnea

Pauses in breathing or shallow breaths during sleep, treated with oral appliances.

Conservative first.Reversible treatments like behavior changes, medication, jaw exercises and orthotic appliances have proven safe and effective in most TMJ cases. Surgery is usually considered only after these have been tried.

Meet your doctor
