Meet your esthetician

Hi, I'm Mikki.

With 13 years of experience and counting, I offer a no-nonsense, honest approach to skin care. I help with active acne, acne scarring and hyperpigmentation, and with aging gracefully with healthy, beautiful skin.

Stress, the environment, nutrition and products all affect your skin, so we talk about all of it. There's a lot of conflicting information out there. If I don't know an answer, I'll research until I find it.
