02 / Meet the dentist

Whatever bridge you need, he has you covered.

Dr. McAdams grew up in Fairbanks, Alaska, where he learned to love the outdoors and fly fishing. He earned a civil engineering degree at Montana State University and built roads and runways in Phoenix before his wife Jaime, the practice's dental hygienist, inspired him to switch careers.

  • 2001Civil Engineering degree, Montana State University, then his Professional Engineer license
  • Dental schoolArthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, University of the Pacific, San Francisco
  • 2011Opened McAdams Dental back home in Arizona
  • Implant trainingCompleted the AAID Maxicourse, a 300-hour mini residency in dental implants
