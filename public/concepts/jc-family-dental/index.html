Meet your dentist

Dr. Jae Chung, DDS

Dr. Chung grew up in Kansas. After working in the dental field for a few years, he pursued his dream and earned his dental degree at the University of Missouri-Kansas City. He prides himself on educating patients about their oral health so they can make the best decisions about their treatment.

Services

Gentle care, from check-ups to crowns.

Modern technology, regular training and a close eye on dental research, so your teeth get expert treatment in a relaxed setting.
