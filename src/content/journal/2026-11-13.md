Today I'm starting a website for a taco shop. It's an example for now: an imaginary
taquería called El Único that sells one taco, three aguas frescas and two desserts,
chocoflan and tres leches cake. The idea is to show what I could build for a real
restaurant.

I'm really impressed by the state of AI compared to back in March. Vibe coding is way
easier now.
